## Privacy policy for Rental Intel

Rental Intel does not collect, transmit, or share any data or personal
information. There is no account, no server, and no analytics or telemetry.

The extension does nothing on any website until you enable that site on
its Sources tab, at which point Chrome asks you for access to that site
alone. On an enabled site, when you click **Add listing** in the side panel
while viewing a rental listing, the listing's details and photos are copied
into the extension's local storage in your browser, on your device only.
The only network requests the extension makes are to fetch a listing's photos
from the site's own image servers, and only in response to that click.
Nothing is stored from pages you merely visit.

Your saved data leaves your device only if you choose **Save to file…** or
**Copy to clipboard** on the side panel's Data tab. Disabling a site on the
Sources tab gives its access back to Chrome; uninstalling the extension
deletes all of its stored data.

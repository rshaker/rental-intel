## Privacy policy for Rental Intel

Rental Intel does not collect, transmit, or share any data or personal
information. There is no account, no server, and no analytics or telemetry.

Listings you save are stored in your browser, on your device only. The
extension's only network requests fetch a saved listing's photos from the
listing site itself.

Your saved data leaves your device only if you export it yourself.
Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored.

About Marq Uptown

Live close to class, work, and everything Austin at Marq Uptown. Located along I-35 in Central Austin, our pet-friendly studio, 1-, and 2-bedroom apartments place you within walking distance of The University of Texas at Austin and minutes from downtown offices, hospitals, music venues, restaurants, and nightlife. Inside, enjoy modern interiors with granite countertops, spacious walk-in closets, and comfortable layouts designed for busy city living. When you're home, recharge with community amenities like a sparkling pool, 24-hour fitness center, onsite yoga studio, and inviting social spaces. Whether you're heading to campus, commuting downtown, catching a show, or meeting friends for dinner, Marq Uptown gives you an easy home base in one of Austin's most connected locations.

Marq Uptown is an apartment community located in Travis County and the 78705 ZIP Code. This area is served by the Austin Independent School District attendance zone.
